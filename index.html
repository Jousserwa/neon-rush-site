// Chapter 01 · Fight Back

A gun that grows with the danger

When hazards stack up, a WEAPON SURGE button flashes for two seconds. Tap it and your gun adds another stream of shots, each one locking onto its own target.

  • 2 hazards attacking at once: twin guns, +25% damage.
  • 3 hazards: three streams, +50%.
  • 4 hazards: four streams, +75%.
  • 5 hazards: five streams, +100%.
  • One volley still costs one shot. Against a boss the volley is capped, so air strikes stay your real answer.
  • Between big waves you get breathing room: each extra hazard attacking at once makes new ones arrive 10 percent less often.
WEAPON SURGE · TRY IT
Pick a step and watch every stream pick its own target.

ARMOR MAGAZINES

Collect armor on the track and it stays loaded in your gun. Armor rounds hit harder and one blast wipes out a boss brood.

BOMBS

Wipe out boss spawn and shake the boss with the BOMB button. Keep a pocket of bombs and a reserve behind it.

MILITARY SHIELD

The rare gold shield takes 10 hits, then keeps protecting you for 4 more seconds. Make them count.

// Chapter 03 · The Finale

Take down the finale boss

A boss arrives every few zones, now with escort ships guarding it. Beat the escorts, break the shield, and when the last boss of a world falls it does not just disappear.

  • The boss shudders white-hot, then bursts into shining pieces of its own picture, in slow motion.
  • You cannot be hurt while the victory plays, so there is time to watch it.
  • The reward: 2 Military Shields, 2 airstrikes, 2 bombs and 10 gems.
  • Every species you destroy ranks up in the Species Codex.

// Chapter 04 · Phase Gate

A crack opens in your lane

Every 8 to 14 minutes of flying, a portal drifts toward you in the middle of a run. Fly in and the whole screen becomes another realm for a few seconds. You keep flying, catch up to three gems and slip past floating obstacles. Then you snap back.

CRYSTAL SEA
🌀

ZONE ECHO

Some gates drop you into a few seconds of a far zone like 30 or 50, with its real hazards, real speed and its guardian boss. Nothing counts and nothing is lost.

💎

GIFT GEMS

Steer to catch the gems floating through the realm: up to three per gate, and a PERFECT for slipping past every obstacle.

🛟

ALWAYS SAFE

You cannot be hurt inside a gate. It waits for a calm moment: no boss, no airstrike, never while paused.

// Chapter 05 · Call For Backup

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what information Neon Rush: Jetpack Space Run (the "Game") collects, why, and what choices you have. The Game is published by JerussFly Technologies ("we", "us"). By playing the Game you agree to this policy.

In short: we do not ask for your name, address or phone number, and we do not sell your personal information. We use standard services (Google, Firebase, RevenueCat, Google AdMob) to run the Game, save progress, process purchases, show ads and fix crashes.

1. Information we collect

Gameplay and progress. Your scores, gems, upgrades, unlocked items, settings and run history. Most of this is stored on your device. Some is stored online so your progress and leaderboards work.

Account and sign-in. If you sign in with Google Play Games, we receive your Play Games player name and ID to save progress and show leaderboards. We do not see your Google password. The Game can also create an anonymous account identifier so your data can be saved without signing in.

Device and usage data. Device model, Android version, language, app version, crash reports and diagnostics, and how you use features of the Game. This is collected automatically through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.

Advertising ID. The Game may use your device's advertising ID so ads can be served and measured. You can reset or delete it in your Android settings.

Purchases. When you buy something in the Game, the payment is handled by Google Play. We receive confirmation of what you bought (not your card details), through Google Play Billing and RevenueCat, so we can unlock your items and restore them later.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, the Game may send reminders such as daily rewards. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.

2. How we use information

  • To run the Game, save and restore your progress, and provide leaderboards.
  • To process and restore purchases.
  • To show ads and measure how well they work.
  • To find and fix bugs and crashes, and to improve the Game.
  • To protect the Game from cheating and abuse.

3. Services we use

These providers process data on our behalf or under their own policies:

  • Google Play Services / Play Games (sign-in, leaderboards, in-app review): policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Authentication, Firestore): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob (ads): policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • RevenueCat (purchase management): revenuecat.com/privacy

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers above, when required by law, or to protect our rights and the safety of players.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your choices and your data

  • Ads: reset or opt out of ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Notifications: turn off in your device settings.
  • Delete your data: contact us from the address below and we will delete the online data linked to your account. Uninstalling the Game removes the data stored on your device.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data. Contact us to use them.

7. Data retention and security

We keep data only as long as needed to run the Game, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. We use reasonable measures to protect data, but no system is completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continuing to play after a change means you accept it.
